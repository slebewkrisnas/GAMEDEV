MISSION BRIEFING & CONTROLS

TACTICAL OBJECTIVE

You are stranded in an abandoned quarantine zone. Eliminate all hostile infected across 3 consecutive waves to trigger extraction.

COMBAT CONTROLS

  • W A S D Navigate horizontal space
  • SPACE Tactical Jump / Vault
  • SHIFT Tactical Sprint
  • F Toggle Tactical Flashlight
  • MOUSE Look and Aim reticle
  • LEFT CLICK Discharge 9mm Pistol
  • R Tactical Reload (1.3s)
  • ESC Pause simulation / Release cursor

COMBAT INTEL

CRITICAL WEAKNESS: Aim for the head! Headshots inflict fatal 1-shot critical damage. Body shots require 3 hits.

WAVE PROGRESSION: Wave 1 features slow walkers. Wave 2 deploys fast sprinters. Wave 3 unleashes heavy brutes and fast sprinters.
